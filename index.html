01 — About

About the Project

The Metacrisis is a conceptual frame for capturing civilisation's total problem complex, the generative dynamics that (re)produce it and the attractor states it vectors towards. It marks the terminal phase in the developmental process of the current world-system, which is now emergently posing an existential risk to humanity.

This map assembles that frame into a single, explorable structure: a nested hierarchy of crises and their causes, a set of diagrams tracing how the generative dynamics interlock and reinforce one another, and a curated landscape of the research shaping how we understand and respond to it.

It is not a finished argument but a living reference, built to be explored non-linearly, from the whole down to any single concept, or from any single concept back out to the whole.

03 — User Guide

User Guide

  • Navigate. Use the menu in the bottom-left corner to move between the Main tree, Map, Module, Maximal, Problems, Generators, Attractors and Landscape views.
  • Expand the tree. On Main, click the title to open the menu, then click through each level of concepts. A back arrow always returns you one level up.
  • Read entries. Any underlined concept name opens its full glossary entry, including sources and references where available.
  • Explore visually. Map lets you pan and zoom the entire tree as a node diagram; Maximal lays every entry out on one continuous page.
  • Theme & accent. The diamond toggles light and dark mode; the ring cycles the accent colour used for interactive highlights — try the live demo alongside.
  • Keyboard shortcuts. Press 1–2 to switch theme, 3–7 to cycle accent colours, and F to toggle the serif typeface.

Generative Dynamics: Verticals

A hierarchical mapping of the structural traps driving civilisation towards catastrophe.
Fourth-Order
Exponential Techno-Economic Development

The continuous, compounding acceleration of global economic throughput and technological power amplification acts as a planetary multiplier, drastically raising the stakes and speed of all underlying structural traps. It transforms historically localized failure modes into immediate, inescapable global catastrophic risks.

Third-Order
The AI Arms Race

Competing actors race to develop AGI to avoid losing global strategic dominance, structurally sidelining safety protocols and ethical guardrails in favor of rapid deployment. This accelerates existential risk trajectories as unaligned models saturate critical infrastructure.

The Environmental Tragedy of the Commons

Individual actors rationally maximize extraction and fossil-fuel combustion for short-term profit, while the cumulative ecological externalities are distributed globally, destabilizing the Earth's biosphere. No single actor can unilaterally halt extraction without suffering immediate economic suicide.

Second-Order
The Multi-polar Trap

A game-theoretic dynamic where individual rational incentives directly conflict with collective wellbeing locks actors into a race to the bottom, compelling them to defect even when cooperation is globally optimal. This structural inevitability makes solving global-scale coordination problems mathematically hostile.

First-Order
Super-Structural Separation

The global political economy organizes humanity into deeply separated collective agents—nation-states and corporations—as its fundamental structural units, legitimized by the cultural software of nationalism and tribalism that hijacks evolutionary dispositions like in/out-group bias. These impermeable self/other boundaries act as the structural and phenomenological "walls" that artificially divide agents, hard-coding the prisoner's dilemma into civilisation.

Cross-Cutting Dynamic
& Effect
Perverse Incentive Structures

Economic and structural frameworks systematically reward extractive and harmful behaviors over regenerative action, entirely externalizing the costs of ecological and social degradation so they fail to impact the optimizing agent. Good actors are competitively disadvantaged and quickly driven out of the ecosystem by ruthless systemic forces.

Negative Externalities

The inevitable result of maximizing a narrow proxy metric is the creation of unforeseen harm in the broader system, since the proxy cannot capture the full complexity of reality and optimized systems systematically degrade unmeasured dimensions. These consequences accumulate silently outside the system's awareness until they trigger catastrophic, systemic failures.

Instantiations
National Growth (GDP)

States ruthlessly optimize for Gross Domestic Product to maintain geopolitical power, aggressively incentivizing environmental extraction and resource consumption. Steady-state or degrowth economics are rejected as total strategic suicide.

Corporate Profit (MCAP)

Fiduciary duty mandates the continuous maximization of shareholder value, making monopolistic behavior and aggressive market capture structurally necessary. Complex social value is collapsed into single-dimensional financial returns.

Algorithmic Attention (TOS)

Tech platforms optimize exclusively for Time-On-Site to maximize ad revenue, with content algorithms hijacking evolutionary psychology using outrage and tribalism. This inevitably degrades public sensemaking and drastically polarizes populations.

Technological Efficiency

Tech platforms and engineering maximize for pure computational or energy efficiency, but rebound effects (Jevons paradox) completely offset efficiency gains with proportionally higher consumption. Broader systemic resilience and critical redundancy are sacrificed to maximize pure throughput.

Non-profits & Charity Initiatives

Organizations optimize for donor-pleasing KPIs like outcome achievement rates and raw beneficiary reach, so focus inevitably narrows to measurable, short-term interventions over complex, systemic paradigm change. Deep root causes of suffering are structurally ignored to maintain steady, attractive metric reporting.

Educational Results (GPA)

Educational systems optimize for standardized testing and measurable proxies, discarding holistic human development, civic wisdom, and ethical reasoning. Society produces highly competent technicians lacking broader systemic context.

Second-Order
Narrow Metric Optimisation

The systemic imperative to infinitely maximize singular proxies over holistic reality blindly sacrifices unmeasured parameters that are often essential for systemic health. This creates "Goodhart's Law" scenarios where the proxy completely destroys the original goal.

First-Order
Isolated Problem-Solving

Institutions attack singular problems without grasping the deeply interconnected whole, so specialized "solutions" routinely become the generative source of new, worse meta-crises. Ontological separation fully prevents the coordination required for systemic paradigm shifts.

Metacrisis & Complex Systems Theory
The Three Attractors Model (Schmachtenberger)
Daniel Schmachtenberger's proposal that any sufficiently complex, competition-driven civilisation optimising for relative power across multiple uncoordinated actors trends toward one of three basic attractor states – catastrophic multipolar failure, centralised authoritarian control, or a deliberately chosen synergistic order – the direct conceptual source for this section's own three-attractor framework.
Existential Risk Studies & Macrostrategy
The Vulnerable World Hypothesis (Bostrom)
Nick Bostrom's proposal that if technological development continues indefinitely, civilisation will eventually draw a "black ball" from the urn of possible inventions – one whose default outcome is civilisational devastation – unless extraordinary preventive policing and governance capacity are developed first.
Rogue Actor Catastrophes: The mode of risk this black ball creates – where cheap, widely accessible technology lets a single malicious individual or small group inflict civilisation-scale harm, without needing the resources of a state.
High-Tech Panopticon: Bostrom's proposed safeguard against rogue actor catastrophes – a regime of pervasive, real-time surveillance and preventive policing capable of detecting and interdicting catastrophic misuse before it happens, at the cost of an unprecedented and potentially permanent surveillance state.
Liberal Political Theory
The Narrow Corridor (Acemoglu, Robinson)
Daron Acemoglu and James Robinson's thesis that liberty survives only within a narrow corridor of continuous, dynamic balance between state capacity and societal mobilisation – too weak a state invites domination by private actors, too strong a state invites despotism.
Absent Leviathan: A weak or absent state, unable to enforce order or provide public goods, leaving society exposed to domination by warlords, private hierarchies and organised violence in the vacuum of central authority.
Despotic Leviathan: A state whose power vastly outstrips that of society, able to dominate and control the population unchecked – producing order through domination rather than liberty.
Shackled Leviathan: The corridor's ideal outcome – a state strong enough to provide order and public goods, but held in check by an equally mobilised and vigilant society, generating liberty through perpetual contest rather than either extreme.
Classical Political Philosophy
Anacyclosis: Cycle of Political Evolution (Plato, Aristotle, Polybius)
The classical theory, developed by Plato, elaborated by Aristotle, and systematised by Polybius, that every political regime passes through a fixed, repeating cycle of constitutional forms – rule by one, by the few, and by the many, each rising in virtue and decaying into its corrupt counterpart – so that a civilisation's political order is a transitional stage in a recurring cycle rather than a stable endpoint.
Monarchy → Tyranny: Kingship – rule by a single virtuous ruler governing for the common good – degenerates into tyranny, where one ruler pursues self-interest and dominates the population without restraint.
Aristocracy → Oligarchy: Aristocracy – rule by the most virtuous and capable minority – degenerates into oligarchy, where a wealthy or privileged few govern for their own benefit rather than the common good.
Democracy → Ochlocracy: Democracy – rule by an informed, public-spirited citizenry – degenerates into ochlocracy, or mob rule, where the majority is swayed by demagogues into short-sighted factionalism, from which a strongman typically restores monarchy and the cycle begins again.
