  • The Metacrisis

    The Metacrisis is a conceptual frame for capturing civilisation’s total problem complex, the generative dynamics that (re)produce it and the attractor states its vectors towards. It marks the terminal phase in the developmental process of the current world-system, which is now emergently posing an existential risk to humanity.

    • Problem Complex

      The interconnected web of global crises—ecological, economic, and social—that cannot be isolated or resolved independently without triggering cascading effects.

      • Ecological Breakdown

        Destabilisation of planetary boundaries, rapid biodiversity loss, and critical resource depletion.

      • Socio-Economic Decay

        Extreme wealth stratification, systemic institutional failure, and structural fragility.

      • Meaning Crisis

        The erosion of shared truth, cultural coherence, and our collective capacity for sense-making.

    • Generative Dynamics

      The underlying systems, incentive structures, cultural paradigms, and cognitive biases that continuously drive, exacerbate, and reproduce these crises.

      • Exponential Tech

        Unregulated power scaling of technology without the corresponding wisdom to steward it safely.

      • Rivalrous Traps

        Win-lose game theory structures that incentivize extraction over large-scale coordination.

      • Epistemic Fracturing

        The algorithmic degradation of information ecosystems polarizing shared reality.

    • Attractor States

      The inevitable future conditions, ranging from systemic collapse to radical paradigm transformation, that current trajectories are gravitationally pulling civilisation towards.

      • Systemic Collapse

        The uncontrolled fragmentation and cascading failure of global coordination mechanisms.

      • Totalitarianism

        The application of draconian surveillance and AI control to artificially force system stability.

      • Phase Shift

        The emergence of a higher-order, symbiotic socio-economic paradigm solving root issues.
