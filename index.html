Generative Dynamics

A hierarchical mapping of the structural traps driving civilisation towards catastrophe.
Fourth-Order
Exponential Techno-Economic Development

The continuous, compounding acceleration of global economic throughput and technological power amplification acts as a planetary multiplier, drastically raising the stakes and speed of all underlying structural traps. It transforms historically localized failure modes into immediate, inescapable global catastrophic risks.

Third-Order
The AI Arms Race

Competing actors race to develop AGI to avoid losing global strategic dominance, structurally sidelining safety protocols and ethical guardrails in favor of rapid deployment. This accelerates existential risk trajectories as unaligned models saturate critical infrastructure.

The Environmental Tragedy of the Commons

Individual actors rationally maximize extraction and fossil-fuel combustion for short-term profit, while the cumulative ecological externalities are distributed globally, destabilizing the Earth's biosphere. No single actor can unilaterally halt extraction without suffering immediate economic suicide.

Second-Order
The Multi-polar Trap

A game-theoretic dynamic where individual rational incentives directly conflict with collective wellbeing locks actors into a race to the bottom, compelling them to defect even when cooperation is globally optimal. This structural inevitability makes solving global-scale coordination problems mathematically hostile.

First-Order
Super-Structural Separation

The global political economy organizes humanity into deeply separated collective agents—nation-states and corporations—as its fundamental structural units, legitimized by the cultural software of nationalism and tribalism that hijacks evolutionary dispositions like in/out-group bias. These impermeable self/other boundaries act as the structural and phenomenological "walls" that artificially divide agents, hard-coding the prisoner's dilemma into civilisation.

Cross-Cutting Dynamic
& Effect
Perverse Incentive Structures

Economic and structural frameworks systematically reward extractive and harmful behaviors over regenerative action, entirely externalizing the costs of ecological and social degradation so they fail to impact the optimizing agent. Good actors are competitively disadvantaged and quickly driven out of the ecosystem by ruthless systemic forces.

Negative Externalities

The inevitable result of maximizing a narrow proxy metric is the creation of unforeseen harm in the broader system, since the proxy cannot capture the full complexity of reality and optimized systems systematically degrade unmeasured dimensions. These consequences accumulate silently outside the system's awareness until they trigger catastrophic, systemic failures.

Instantiations
National Growth (GDP)

States ruthlessly optimize for Gross Domestic Product to maintain geopolitical power, aggressively incentivizing environmental extraction and resource consumption. Steady-state or degrowth economics are rejected as total strategic suicide.

Corporate Profit (MCAP)

Fiduciary duty mandates the continuous maximization of shareholder value, making monopolistic behavior and aggressive market capture structurally necessary. Complex social value is collapsed into single-dimensional financial returns.

Algorithmic Attention (TOS)

Tech platforms optimize exclusively for Time-On-Site to maximize ad revenue, with content algorithms hijacking evolutionary psychology using outrage and tribalism. This inevitably degrades public sensemaking and drastically polarizes populations.

Technological Efficiency

Tech platforms and engineering maximize for pure computational or energy efficiency, but rebound effects (Jevons paradox) completely offset efficiency gains with proportionally higher consumption. Broader systemic resilience and critical redundancy are sacrificed to maximize pure throughput.

Non-profits & Charity Initiatives

Organizations optimize for donor-pleasing KPIs like outcome achievement rates and raw beneficiary reach, so focus inevitably narrows to measurable, short-term interventions over complex, systemic paradigm change. Deep root causes of suffering are structurally ignored to maintain steady, attractive metric reporting.

Educational Results (GPA)

Educational systems optimize for standardized testing and measurable proxies, discarding holistic human development, civic wisdom, and ethical reasoning. Society produces highly competent technicians lacking broader systemic context.

Second-Order
Narrow Metric Optimisation

The systemic imperative to infinitely maximize singular proxies over holistic reality blindly sacrifices unmeasured parameters that are often essential for systemic health. This creates "Goodhart's Law" scenarios where the proxy completely destroys the original goal.

First-Order
Isolated Problem-Solving

Institutions attack singular problems without grasping the deeply interconnected whole, so specialized "solutions" routinely become the generative source of new, worse meta-crises. Ontological separation fully prevents the coordination required for systemic paradigm shifts.
